# What makes a carrier worth chasing?

The live weapons are the README rules: cannon, missile, and drone chips, four levels each, and a reserve drone once all three tracks are full. This note keeps the references that led there, and the first controller-flight experiment that the current chips replaced.

## M.U.S.H.A. / Musha Aleste

Power chips strengthen the main shot. Every third chip also earns a drone. Two drones can fly at once, with extras held in reserve to replace losses. Six formations give those drones different jobs. Reverse fires opposite the direction of movement, so a dodge can double as an attack. A separate set of pickups equips a beam, bomb or barrier. Repeating the equipped type raises its level. Taking a hit can cost the special weapon while preserving its level for a later pickup. [Mega Drive manual, pp. 8–11](https://www.oldiesrising.com/noticespdfV2/Sega%20Megadrive/Musha%20Aleste.pdf)

## Blazing Lazers

Weapon choice changes which part of the screen the player controls. Power Wave grows wider, Field Thunder changes its path, and Ring Blaster adds protective rings. Separate enhancements include homing missiles, a shield and a follower that retraces the player's movements. Common purple gels advance the equipped weapon. The manual gives successive costs of 1, 2, 4, 8, 12 and 16 gels. The first pickup pays off immediately. Later levels give the player a reason to cross a dangerous lane for another gel. [TurboGrafx-16 manual, pp. 4–5, 9](https://dds.konami.com/games/manual/pcemini/en_Blazing.pdf)

## First experiment in the controller-flight preview

The first green chip equips one hollow vector wing pod. The pod adds an offset bolt on the existing fire cadence. Make the extra firing lane narrow enough that the player still needs to aim. Keep the bullet sound to one cue per volley. With a carrier every ten seconds, requiring three clean pickups before hearing or seeing a weapon change would leave much of a short playtest empty.

The second chip equips the left pod and immediately tips both pods outward by 12°. Chip three adds two homing missiles to the spread. Each later chip adds one missile per pod, giving four missiles at chip four and six at chip five. Each extra pair opens the outer launch angle by 7° up to 75°. Missiles coast outward for 0.28 seconds before homing, accelerate from 145 to 480 logical pixels per second and leave a half-second mint trail. Each volley shares one launch sound and the existing 0.84-second cooldown.

During a crossing wave, try clearing a scout with an outside lane while keeping the center bolt trained on the carrier. On iPad, check whether the wider gaps still leave the incoming magenta round easy to track.

Offensive drones share a lock when only one opponent is visible and spread across targets when more appear. Defensive drones continue to intercept separate projectiles. Drone targeting scans on alternate gameplay updates. A short rising chirp marks a new lock, with simultaneous locks sounding together.
